Leading the Standard in Petroleum Transportation

Zero Waste Haulage & Logistics Limited, operating as Zero Waste Petroleum Transport Co, is a premier oil and gas transportation company headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria. Founded and led by experienced power infrastructure engineers, we bring technical precision and safety excellence to every haul.

Our commitment to zero waste and zero incidents drives everything we do. We combine industry-leading safety protocols with modern fleet management and real-time tracking to ensure your petroleum products arrive safely, on time, every time.

With our strategic location in Abuja and nationwide coverage, we serve major oil marketers, independent dealers, and industrial clients across Nigeria's oil and gas sector.

Comprehensive Petroleum Transportation Solutions

Bulk Petroleum Haulage

Industrial Supply Services
